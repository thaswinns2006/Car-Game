📚 Small Changes, Big Impact

Learn how everyday choices shape environmental sustainability.

🧪 Fundamentals

What is CO₂e?

Carbon dioxide equivalent (CO₂e) expresses the impact of various greenhouse gases (methane, nitrous oxide) in terms of the amount of CO₂ that would create the same warming effect.

🚘 Mobility

Why Transport Matters

Passenger vehicles account for over 45% of global transport carbon emissions. Active travel or public transit dramatically drops individual emissions per kilometer.

🥑 Nutrition

Food & Agriculture

Red meat production produces nearly 5x the emissions of poultry and 10x that of legumes. Shifting even 2 meals a week to plant-based options has immediate benefits.

💡 Energy

Home Energy Efficiency

Electricity grids still rely heavily on fossil fuels. Unplugging phantom electronics and optimizing thermostatic controls reduces grid load and utility bills.
